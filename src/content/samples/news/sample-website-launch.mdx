> **Sample article.** This post is a development placeholder and is excluded from production builds. Replace it with a real announcement in `src/content/news/`.

The news section supports MDX, so articles can mix prose with lists, tables, links and code.

## What you can include

- Lab updates and announcements
- Research notes and explainers
- Publication and conference highlights
- Calls for participation

## Formatting

Headings, **bold text**, `inline code` and [links](/research) are all supported, as are fenced code blocks:

```ts
const lab = "STEADI Lab";
```
