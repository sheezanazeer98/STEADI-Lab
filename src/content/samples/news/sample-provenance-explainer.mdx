Provenance records answer a simple question about any result: _where did this come from?_ For machine learning that means the dataset version, the preprocessing steps, the training configuration and the evaluation procedure.

## The verification gap

When those records are kept informally they are easy to lose and impossible for others to check. Anchoring their digests on a tamper-evident ledger closes the gap without requiring the underlying data to be shared.
