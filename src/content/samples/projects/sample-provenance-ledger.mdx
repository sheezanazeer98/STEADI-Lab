## Technical approach

The system intercepts dataset loads, training runs and evaluation jobs through lightweight SDK hooks, producing a lineage graph for every result. Cryptographic digests of each artefact are anchored on a permissioned ledger, while the artefacts themselves remain in institutional storage.

A verification service reconstructs the lineage of any published figure and checks every digest against the ledger, producing a human-readable evidence report.
