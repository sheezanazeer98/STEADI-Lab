Smart contracts execute automatically and are difficult to change once deployed, which makes correctness and security critical. This area develops techniques to analyse contracts before deployment and to monitor them afterwards.

## Focus

Our work combines classical program analysis with learning-based methods. Static analysers and formal tools provide guarantees for specific properties; machine-learning models help prioritise review effort and recognise patterns across large corpora of deployed code. We are especially interested in making verification results **understandable and actionable** for developers.

## Outputs

Techniques from this area feed directly into the lab's open-source tooling and into the analysis of decentralised applications.
