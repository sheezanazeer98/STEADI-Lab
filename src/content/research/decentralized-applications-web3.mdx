Decentralised applications combine smart contracts, distributed storage and client software into systems that no single party controls. This area examines how such applications should be designed, where they add value, and what obstacles stand between prototypes and dependable products.

## Focus

We take a pragmatic view of Web3: decentralisation is a tool, not a goal. Projects in this area investigate verifiable credentials for academic and professional records, decentralised data sharing for research, and interface patterns that make cryptographic systems approachable for everyday users.

## Interdisciplinary links

The work draws on human–computer interaction, economics and law as well as computer science, and connects closely with our research on governance and oversight.
