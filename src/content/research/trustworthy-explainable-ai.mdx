Trust in an AI system is earned through transparency and evidence. This area develops techniques that expose how models reach their conclusions, quantify when they are likely to be wrong, and document their behaviour in a form that can be audited.

## Focus

We investigate interpretability methods for both tabular and language models, fairness measurement across groups and contexts, and robustness against distribution shift and adversarial inputs. A recurring theme is **verifiable evidence** — pairing a model with records that let others check its claims.

## Connection to blockchain research

Tamper-evident ledgers are a natural home for audit trails. Several lab projects explore recording model provenance, evaluation results and decision logs on distributed ledgers so that accountability does not depend on a single party.
