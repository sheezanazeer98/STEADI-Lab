Distributed ledgers provide a shared, tamper-evident record without requiring participants to trust a single operator. This area studies the underlying systems — consensus, networking, storage and cryptography — and how they can be engineered for dependable use.

## Focus

We evaluate ledger designs against realistic workloads, examine interoperability between networks, and explore privacy-preserving techniques that let data be verified without being revealed. Our interest is less in cryptocurrency markets and more in **ledgers as infrastructure** for records, coordination and accountability.

## Relationship to AI

Ledgers offer AI systems a dependable memory: a place to anchor data provenance, model versions and decision logs. Conversely, learning techniques can help monitor networks, detect anomalies and optimise protocol parameters.
