Artificial intelligence and machine learning form the technical core of the lab. We study how models learn useful representations from data, how they can be adapted efficiently to new domains, and how their behaviour can be evaluated honestly.

## Focus

Our work spans classic statistical learning through modern foundation models. Rather than chasing leaderboard scores, we emphasise models whose performance is **measurable, reproducible and stable** under the kinds of change that occur in real deployments — new users, new data sources and new objectives.

## Why it matters

Reliable learning is a prerequisite for every other theme in the lab. Trustworthy AI, agentic systems and intelligent decision support all depend on models that behave predictably and whose limitations are understood.
