Decisions are only as sound as the data and models behind them. This area focuses on making the origin and history of data visible, protecting it from undetected modification, and presenting the result in decision-support tools that help people act with confidence.

## Focus

We develop techniques for capturing provenance across data pipelines, anchoring integrity evidence in distributed ledgers, and designing interfaces that communicate evidence and uncertainty clearly. Application areas include scientific data sharing, institutional records and operational decision-making.

## Why ledgers

A tamper-evident ledger lets multiple parties confirm that a dataset, model or report is exactly what was originally produced, without trusting any one of them to keep the record honest.
