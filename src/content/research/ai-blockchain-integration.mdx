AI and blockchain are often discussed separately. STEADI Lab's defining interest is their combination: using distributed ledgers to make AI systems more transparent and accountable, and using AI to make decentralised systems more capable and secure.

## Integration patterns

Our work explores several recurring patterns:

- **Ledger-anchored AI** — recording datasets, model versions, evaluation results and decisions on a tamper-evident ledger.
- **Verifiable inference** — producing evidence that a particular model produced a particular output.
- **Agents on decentralised rails** — autonomous agents that coordinate and settle commitments through smart contracts under explicit rules.
- **Intelligent protocols** — learning-based monitoring, anomaly detection and parameter tuning for ledger networks.

## Outcome

The goal is an ecosystem of composable, trustworthy components rather than isolated demonstrations — infrastructure that other researchers and practitioners can build on.
