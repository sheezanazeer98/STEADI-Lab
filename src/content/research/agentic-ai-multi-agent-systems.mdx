Agentic AI moves models from answering questions to taking actions. That shift raises new questions about control: what an agent may do, how its actions can be checked, and how several agents can work together safely.

## Focus

We study agent architectures built on large language models, protocols for multi-agent coordination, and mechanisms for bounding agent behaviour. Decentralised infrastructure is a recurring design element — smart contracts and distributed ledgers can act as neutral referees that record commitments and enforce agreed rules between agents.

## Research direction

Our aim is agents whose autonomy is **earned incrementally**: expanding what an agent may do as evidence of reliable behaviour accumulates, and keeping humans in control of consequential decisions.
