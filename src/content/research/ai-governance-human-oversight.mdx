As AI systems take on consequential tasks, the question of who is accountable — and how they stay in control — becomes central. This area studies governance mechanisms that are enforceable in practice, not just described in policy documents.

## Focus

We work on the technical side of governance: logging and audit infrastructure, controls that restrict what automated systems may do, and interfaces that give human reviewers the information they need to intervene. Decentralised ledgers appear here as a way to make audit records **tamper-evident and shared** among the parties responsible for a system.

## Status

This is an emerging area for the lab that builds on our trustworthy-AI and agentic-systems research.
